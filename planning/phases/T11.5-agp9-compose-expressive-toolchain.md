### Risks / pitfalls

- **Stable BOM ≠ Expressive.** 2026.06.x maps material3 **1.4.0**. T09's "BOM 2025.08.01 for Expressive" was the old 1.5.0-alpha-on-stable-BOM story; 1.4 **deleted** those APIs. Must use **alpha BOM or material3 1.5.0-alpha override**.
- **BOM 2026.08.00** may or may not exist on mapping page at execute time. Do not fail the task if Compose is still 1.11.x **as long as** compileSdk 37 + AGP 9.2.x + material3 1.5 alpha + lint green. Prefer 1.12 if the mapping lists it.
- **kotlin-android + AGP 9** → `ApplicationExtensionImpl cannot be cast to BaseExtension`. Remove kotlin-android; do not cargo-cult `android.builtInKotlin=false` unless proven needed.
- **`android.newDsl=true` (AGP 9 default)** — we do not use `applicationVariants`; should be fine. If a plugin breaks, opt out with `android.newDsl=false` and record it.
- **KSP version string** — `2.3.5` not `2.2.21-1.0.xx`.
- **Lint findings after re-enable** — treat as real; fix. Unit-test sources: keep `checkTestSources = false`.
- **Studio** older than Panda may refuse to open the project; CLI still works.
- **Missing `android-37` platform** — Blocked, not "lower compileSdk".
- **16KB / libzt** — app AGP bump should not require libzt rebuild; if `lib/arm64-v8a` vanishes, check packaging not CMake.
- **Room 2.6.1 + KSP 2.3** — if ksp task fails, bump Room to the smallest 2.7.x that documents KSP 2.3; no schema migration expected (exportSchema false).
